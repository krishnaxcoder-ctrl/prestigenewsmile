## WHO IS THE TREATMENT FOR?

Cosmetic teeth whitening is aimed at **adults (18 years and older)** wishing to regain a brighter smile and revive the natural radiance of their teeth.

**This treatment is particularly suitable:**

- Teeth yellowed or stained by coffee, tea or tobacco
- To those wishing to revive the brightness of their smile
- To anyone wishing to improve the aesthetics of their smile
- As part of regular cosmetic maintenance

Since every smile is unique, every session is uniquely **personalized** in order to obtain a natural, harmonious result perfectly suited to your initial shade.

---

## Contraindications

For safety reasons, teeth whitening is **not recommended** in the following cases:

- People under 18 years of age
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women
- Untreated cavities
- Infections or inflammation of the gums (gingivitis, periodontitis)
- Significant tooth hypersensitivity
- Presence of crowns, veneers or implants on visible teeth

A **Confidential health questionnaire** is systematically sent before the session in order to verify your eligibility and guarantee a suitable treatment in complete safety.

---

## Important Information

The teeth whitening offered by **Prestige Smile** is a cosmetic, non-medical, and non-invasive treatment. It does not replace a diagnosis or treatment performed by a dental surgeon.

- Results may vary depending on the initial tooth shade, the condition of the enamel, and lifestyle habits.
- A scaling performed within 1 to 2 months prior to the session is recommended in order to optimize the result.
- Temporary tooth or gum sensitivity may occur after the session.

---

## Why Get Your Teeth Whitened?

Over time, teeth can lose their natural shine. Certain habits such as consuming coffee, tea, red wine, or tobacco can gradually alter the color of the enamel.

Cosmetic teeth whitening lightens the natural shade of teeth, resulting in a **visibly whiter, brighter, and more harmonious smile**.

At **Prestige Smile**, the protocol acts on the pigments responsible for discoloration while respecting the enamel and gums.

- A first session allows you to obtain a visible result.
- For a more intense and longer-lasting lightening effect, two sessions spaced apart **48 hours** may be recommended.
- The results can then be maintained on average **between 8 and 12 months**, according to lifestyle habits.

## How a Session Unfolds

### How does your session at Prestige Smile unfold?

Each session is conducted according to a precise protocol, in order to offer you a comfortable, safe experience and optimal results.

**1️⃣ Diagnosis & Eligibility**
Before your appointment, you will be sent a confidential health questionnaire to check for any contraindications. A quick diagnosis is also carried out on site.

**2️⃣ Analysis of the Initial Shade**
The shade of your teeth is assessed using a professional shade guide, in order to determine your starting point and precisely measure the result obtained.

**3️⃣ Gum Preparation and Protection**
A gingival protection is carefully applied to preserve the gums and ensure safe treatment.

**4️⃣ Application of the Whitening Gel**
A professional gel is applied to the teeth to act deeply on the pigments responsible for the discoloration.

**5️⃣ LED Lamp Activation**
The LED lamp activates the gel and allows for gradual and even lightening.

**6️⃣ Results & Comparison**
At the end of the session, the shade is reassessed to concretely observe the lightening achieved.

**⏱ Session duration:** Approximately 1 hour

## FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Is teeth whitening painful?**

The treatment is painless. Some people may experience slight sensitivity during or after the session, but it is temporary and usually disappears within 24 hours.

---

**Are the results visible from the first session?**

Yes, a result is visible from the first session. Depending on the initial shade, a two-session protocol may be recommended for a more intense and lasting result.

---

**How many shades can you gain?**

Teeth can be whitened up to **7 shades**, depending on the initial shade.

---

**How long does the result last?**

The stability of the result depends on the protocol followed and lifestyle habits (coffee, tea, tobacco, etc.).

- After a session, the result generally remains between **6 and 8 months**.
- After a complete protocol (2 sessions), the results can remain on average between **8 and 12 months**, with a more intense and lasting lightening.

To maintain optimal shine over time, a maintenance session can be considered starting from **6 months**, according to your needs.

---

**Does teeth whitening damage teeth?**

No. The protocol used is respectful of enamel and gums when carried out under the prescribed conditions.

---

**Does the treatment work on all teeth?**

Teeth whitening only works on natural teeth. Crowns, veneers and implants do not respond to the treatment.

---

**Should I have my teeth cleaned before the session?**

Yes, descaling is recommended **in the weeks leading up to the session** in order to optimize the results of whitening.

---

**Is it possible to eat or drink after the session?**

Yes, you can eat and drink after your session.

However, during the **24 to 48 hours following treatment**, it is recommended to avoid foods and drinks that may stain teeth, in order to optimize and prolong the result.

**To be limited during this period:**

- Coffee, tea, red wine, sodas and colored drinks
- Red fruits (raspberries, blueberries, cherries…)
- Colorful sauces (tomato, ketchup…)
- Pigmented spices (curry, paprika…)
- Tobacco

Acidic foods (lemon, grapefruit, etc.) should also be avoided in order not to sensitize the enamel.

---

**Are there any contraindications?**

Yes. The treatment is not recommended in certain cases (pregnancy, untreated cavities, infections, significant hypersensitivity, etc.). A health questionnaire is systematically sent before the session to ensure your safety.

---

**Who is teeth whitening for?**

The treatment is aimed at adults (18 years and over) wishing to regain a brighter smile and improve the aesthetics of their teeth.

---

**How often can I have my skin bleached?**

An interview session can be considered every **6 to 12 months**, according to your needs and lifestyle.
